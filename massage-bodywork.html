Massage & Bodywork

Experience healing through touch

At Akasha, our massage and bodywork sessions are rooted in both skill and soul. We combine years of experience, advanced training, and deep intuition to create personalized treatments that truly make a difference. Whether you’re dealing with chronic pain, recovering from injury, or simply in need of deep rest, our work is guided by a genuine commitment to your well-being.

Our offerings include full-body massage, deep tissue, Swedish, cupping therapy (often combined with Gua Sha), lymphatic drainage, hot stone, prenatal, and sports massage. We also integrate supportive tools such as localized infrared light therapy to enhance circulation, ease muscular tension, and support the body’s natural healing response.

Each session is tailored to your body’s needs, blending therapeutic technique with mindful presence to support long-term, pain relief and relaxation.

Clients return to us again and again because they feel the care in our hands and the results in their bodies. We don’t believe in one size fits all. Every treatment is a unique experience designed to help you feel better, move more freely, and reconnect with your body’s natural rhythm.
