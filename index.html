Meet Rosa – Founder of Akasha Holistic Health & Wellness

Rosa Silk is the founder of Akasha Holistic Health & Wellness and a Holistic Health Professional with over 17 years of experience in the healing arts. Beginning her path as a Licensed Massage Therapist, Rosa’s work has evolved into a deeply integrative approach that blends bodywork, energy medicine, and holistic coaching.
With a Master’s degree in Integrative Health and a lifelong passion for wellness, Rosa is devoted to helping her clients release stress, restore balance, and feel at home in their bodies. Her warm and grounded presence creates a safe space for transformation. Whether through massage, cupping, infrared therapy, or deeper holistic practices.
At the heart of her mission is a simple truth: healing is not only about relieving pain, but also about reconnecting with your wholeness, vitality, and authentic self.
