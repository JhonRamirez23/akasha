Meet Rosa – Founder Of Akasha Holistic Health & Wellness

Rosa Silk is the founder and visionary behind Akasha Holistic Health & Wellness and an Integrative Health Professional with over 17 years of experience in the healing arts.
Her work blends advanced bodywork, energy medicine, and holistic health coaching. Creating integrative sessions designed to enhance wellbeing and improve overall quality of life.
With a Master’s degree in Integrative Health and a lifelong dedication to wellness, Rosa helps clients restore balance, cultivate awareness, and make empowered changes that support lasting transformation.
At the heart of her mission lies a simple truth:
Healing is not only about feeling better — it’s about living better, with greater vitality, clarity, and connection to one’s authentic self.
